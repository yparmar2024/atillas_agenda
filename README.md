# Attila’s Agenda

Attila’s Agenda is a student productivity app inspired by Stevens’ duck mascot, Attila. It brings task planning and focused study time into a welcoming workspace, with an animated duck companion to keep students company.

## The experience

- **Workspace:** Organize tasks into To do, In progress, and Done, and move them as work progresses. Attila studies alongside you.
- **Focus room:** Start a timed study session or short break in a scenic setting where Attila can idle, walk, and celebrate your progress. The timer continues when switching between views; refreshing or closing the page resets it.
- **Points:** Earn points by completing tasks and timed sessions. Future rewards will let students personalize Attila with accessories such as hats and sunglasses.

The goal is a polished, accessible experience that makes it easy to start working without overwhelming students with features. New workspaces start empty, with no fabricated tasks or activity.

## Current scope

The starting version includes a personal task board, a focus timer, points, and an animated duck companion. Progress currently lasts only for the open visit and resets when the page is refreshed or closed.

Saved progress and sign-in are planned for a later stage. Mascot accessories, notes, shared boards, and interactions between students’ mascots are also future features. See [TODO.md](TODO.md) for upcoming work.
